2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The majority of features in the App — including offline reading of PDF guides, questions, formulas, and syllabus overviews — can be accessed without submitting any personally identifiable information (PII).

2.1 Information Collected Automatically for Functionality

  • Device Identifiers (Anonymous ID / Android ID): Used exclusively to bind your earned in-app rewards, coin wallet, quiz progress, and badges to your device without requiring you to register a personal account, phone number, or email.
  • Push Notification Registration Token (FCM): Generated by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send optional notices (such as exam routine announcements, new model tests, or result dates).
  • Technical & Diagnostic Logs: When connecting to our server to download new study resources, our servers log standard technical requests (IP address, device model, operating system version, and error diagnostics) to maintain stability and prevent abuse.
  • Approximate Location (Network/IP Level): Derived from your IP address to display regionally relevant educational content and board notices (e.g., Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi). We do not request or collect precise GPS coordinates.

2.2 Optional Information Provided by You

  • Quiz Profile Name & Avatar (Optional): If you choose to personalize your quiz experience, you may set an educational nickname and select an avatar. This is displayed on the public student leaderboard.
  • Community Discussion Posts (Optional): Questions and study tips you voluntarily share on the student discussion board are visible to other users. We moderate all submissions to ensure a safe learning environment.
No Sensitive Permissions Required
The App does not access your Contacts, Microphone, Camera, GPS Fine Location, SMS, or Call Logs. All core educational guidebooks work completely offline once downloaded.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising SDKs

To provide educational guidebooks, model tests, and server hosting free of charge, the App integrates vetted, industry-standard third-party SDKs:

3.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, native, and rewarded ads. AdMob and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and non-sensitive diagnostic data to serve and measure advertisements.

  • In accordance with Google Play Families Policy, ads served to young learners are age-appropriate and do not track behavioral profiles.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Unity Ads

The App may also utilize Unity Ads for fallback rewarded video and interstitial educational rewards. Unity Ads adheres to GDPR and COPPA privacy standards.

  • Unity Technologies Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3.3 Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics & Analytics)

We use Google Firebase to send study notifications, monitor app crash reports, and analyze performance across Android device versions.

  • Firebase Privacy & Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

As an educational app targeted toward Primary and Junior students preparing for scholarship exams:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (such as real full names, home addresses, or phone numbers) from children under the age of 13.
  • We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • Ad requests sent through AdMob are flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment where applicable to ensure no behavioral ad tracking occurs.
  • Parents or legal guardians who believe their child has inadvertently submitted personal information can contact us at academicschoolnet@gmail.com for immediate deletion.

5. Public Leaderboard & Community Guidelines

The App features a competitive scholarship leaderboard to encourage student motivation. If you participate in daily quizzes:

  • Your chosen nickname, student avatar, earned scholarship points/coins, and badge title are publicly visible to other learners on the leaderboard.
  • Please do not use your real phone number, password, or sensitive details as your public nickname.

6. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion Policy

We retain anonymous diagnostic logs for a maximum of 12 months for security and maintenance. Quiz scores and coin balances remain active on your device until deleted.

Right to Erase / Request Data Deletion
In full compliance with Google Play's Data Deletion Policy, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your quiz progress, profile name, coins, and associated records:
  • In-App: Open the App → Go to Profile → Tap "Delete My Data / ডাটা মুছে ফেলুন".
  • Web Deletion Portal (No Login Required): Visit our public deletion request form at https://britti.bdfollow.com/delete-data.php.
  • Email: Send a deletion request to academicschoolnet@gmail.com with your in-app nickname.
Deletion requests are processed and permanently wiped from our database within 30 days.
